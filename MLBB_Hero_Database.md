---

## Example: Balmond

| Skill Type | Skill | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Passive | Bloodthirst | Restores HP after killing units/heroes |
| S1 | Soul Lock | Dash, displacement and defense reduction |
| S2 | Cyclone Sweep | Repeated spin damage |
| Ultimate | Lethal Counter | True Damage execute |

### Balmond Mechanics

- Maintain contact during Cyclone Sweep.
- Repeated hits increase damage.
- Soul Lock can reposition Balmond into optimal spin range.
- Lethal Counter scales with target lost HP.
- Ultimate should normally be reserved for an execute window.

---

## Example: Thamuz

| Skill Type | Skill | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Passive | Grand Lord Lava | Enhanced attacks and True Damage Lava Energy |
| S1 | Molten Scythes | Damage, slow and scythe control |
| S2 | Chasm Trample | Leap/damage/slow with scythe interaction |
| Ultimate | Cauterant Inferno | Sustain zone, attack speed and regeneration |

### Thamuz Mechanics

- Basic Attacks can trigger delayed Lava Energy.
- S2 can interact with travelling Scythes.
- S1/S2 timing can create a reset.
- Ultimate dramatically increases sustained combat value.
- Basic Attacks should be woven between abilities.

---

## Example: X.Borg

| Skill Type | Skill | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Passive | Firaga Armor | Separate armor state and energy system |
| S1 | Fire Missiles | Sustained burn |
| S2 | Fire Stake | Pulls enemies and collects supplies |
| Ultimate | Last Insanity | Charge, slow and True Damage explosion |

### X.Borg Mechanics

- Firaga Armor changes the way damage is absorbed.
- Armor destruction creates a transition into Armorless State.
- Energy restores the Armor.
- Heat management determines damage output.
- Ultimate timing should account for Armor durability.
- Manual detonation can prevent losing the full-value explosion.

# Drafting

## Pick When

Choose a hero when:

- The enemy lacks a direct counter.
- Your team provides required setup.
- The hero's damage profile fits the enemy.
- The hero can reach its intended power spike.
- The team composition covers its weaknesses.

## Avoid When

Avoid when:

- Enemy has hard counter mechanics.
- Enemy has reliable anti-heal against sustain.
- Enemy has excessive kite potential.
- Your team lacks engage.
- Your team lacks frontline.
- Your team lacks damage follow-up.
